Sovereign Trust Layer

A sovereign trust framework designed to provide verifiable integrity, provenance and chain-of-custody proofs for digital assets.

Features
Ed25519 cryptographic signatures
SHA-256 integrity verification
Tamper-evident ledger
Merkle proof generation
Blockchain anchoring
FastAPI-based API architecture
Vision
 
The Sovereign Trust Layer establishes an independent trust framework for digital assets by providing cryptographic evidence of integrity, provenance and chain-of-custody. The platform does not aim to store, process or replicate confidential business data. Instead, it focuses on verifiable cryptographic proofs, digital signatures, Merkle proofs and blockchain anchoring artifacts that can be independently audited.
 
Digital assets remain under the control of their owners. Only the minimum evidence required to demonstrate authenticity, integrity and provenance is processed by the platform. This privacy-preserving approach significantly reduces data exposure while maintaining strong evidentiary value across organizations, jurisdictions and trust domains.
 
 
The Sovereign Trust Layer follows a proof-centric model.
 
The platform is designed to ingest:
 
- Cryptographic hashes
- Digital signatures
- Integrity metadata
- Merkle proofs
- Timestamp evidence
- Blockchain anchoring records
 
The platform is not intended to store:
 
- Personal identifiable information (PII)
- Medical records
- Human resources data
- Financial records
- Passwords or secrets
- Confidential business content
 
By minimizing data ingestion and focusing on verifiable evidence, the platform reduces regulatory, privacy and cybersecurity risks while preserving auditability.
